# Puzzle Picnic Art Collection Guide

## Purpose

This document describes the eighteen scene illustrations currently included in Puzzle Picnic. It records their subjects, motifs, palettes, compositions, and artistic styles, then draws out the visual principles that connect the collection. It is intended as a practical starting point for commissioning or generating new artwork without narrowing the collection to one technique or one type of story.

The style terms below describe the appearance and visual language of each digital image. References to media such as gouache, felt, clay, or screen printing describe the simulated material character rather than a claim that the original was physically made in that medium.

All current scene files are 1200 × 900 pixel WebP images in `public/scenes/`.

## Collection at a glance

Puzzle Picnic is a collection of optimistic, detail-rich story worlds for children. Most scenes show cooperation, exploration, play, food, animals, or gentle adventure. Even spectacular settings—outer space, prehistory, Arctic ice, mountain racing—remain welcoming rather than dangerous.

The collection has a recognizable shared character:

- Warm, emotionally legible scenes with no threatening conflict.
- A landscape composition filled across the whole frame rather than a single isolated subject.
- Clear foreground, middle-ground, and background areas that reward close looking.
- Saturated but friendly color, usually balanced by a strong environmental hue.
- Rounded forms and readable silhouettes, especially for characters.
- Many small discoveries: flowers, food, birds, fish, toys, lights, buildings, or vehicles.
- Narrative action. Characters are doing something together, not simply posing.
- An unbranded, timeless world without text, logos, watermarks, or recognizable franchise characters.

The set deliberately avoids a single house rendering style. Its unity comes from mood, story structure, colorfulness, and puzzle-friendly detail; its variety comes from subject matter and the visual imitation of different art materials.

## Style families

The current works can be understood as five overlapping families.

| Family | Character | Current examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Painterly storybook worlds | Soft modeling, expressive characters, atmospheric light, and familiar picture-book warmth | Seaside Friends, Moonlight Music, Sky Garden, Aurora Camp, Bumble Splash |
| Handcrafted and tactile worlds | Visible or implied paper, clay, wool, thread, glass, or miniature-model materials | Coral Carnival, Little Moon Base, Dragon Meadow, Playtime Park, Alpine Express, Rocket Sunrise |
| Print, drawing, and graphic worlds | Flattened space, assertive outlines, limited or designed color, and conspicuous mark-making | Market Morning, Mud Mountain Rally, Pancake Parade, Flavor Mosaic |
| Observational and scenic worlds | More naturalistic space, light, anatomy, objects, or food, while retaining an inviting illustrative finish | Pocket Raceway, Dinosaur Valley, Table for Everyone |
| Abundant ensemble scenes | Many equal points of interest arranged across the image rather than one dominant protagonist | Coral Carnival, Market Morning, Pancake Parade, Table for Everyone, Playtime Park |

These are not strict categories. The most interesting scenes often combine two families—for example, a naturalistic landscape with picture-book characters or a graphic layout with painterly texture.

## Individual artwork catalogue

### Seaside Friends

**Artwork.** An elephant in a sailor cap pilots a small wooden boat carrying a fox, rabbit, and turtle. They cross bright turquoise water toward a red-and-white lighthouse, with a sailboat, islands, seabirds, fish, and coral extending the journey into the distance and below the surface.

**Style.** Classic painterly children's-book illustration. Soft brush texture, gently modeled forms, rounded faces, and a slightly nostalgic nautical palette recall gouache or opaque watercolor. The image favors clarity and charm over realism: scale is playful, expressions are large and readable, and the sea is decorative as well as spatial.

**Visual character.** Cyan water and sky create a broad cool field, while the orange boat, fox, clothing, and lighthouse add warm anchors. The diagonal wake supplies motion; the lighthouse acts as a destination. Above-water and underwater motifs make the whole frame useful puzzle territory.

**Key motifs.** Friendship, voyaging, lighthouse, small boat, coast, marine life, sunny weather.

### Moonlight Music

**Artwork.** A bear drums, a rabbit plays flute, and a hedgehog dances while frogs listen from lily pads. The concert takes place beside a glowing tree house, waterfall, lanterns, luminous mushrooms, flowers, an owl, and a crescent moon.

**Style.** Luminous fantasy storybook painting. Deep blue and teal shadows are punctuated by golden windows, lanterns, firefly-like dots, and jewel-colored mushrooms. Soft fur and foliage coexist with theatrical lighting, producing the feeling of a richly finished bedtime-book spread.

**Visual character.** The musicians make a circular clearing at the center, framed by the tree and river. Repeated pools of warm light guide the eye around an otherwise dark composition. The dense edge detail and distinct character silhouettes suit close puzzle inspection.

**Key motifs.** Woodland community, music, moonlight, tree house, magical lights, mushrooms, pond life.

### Sky Garden

**Artwork.** A child flies an orange propeller plane past floating garden islands while a red panda waters flowers and vegetables. Windmills, waterfalls, bridges, a gazebo, birds, butterflies, balloons, clouds, fruit trees, and a rainbow turn the sky into an inhabited garden landscape.

**Style.** High-key whimsical fantasy illustration with a painterly storybook finish. Puffy clouds, bright flowers, softened edges, and rounded architecture create an airy dream world. Unlike the nocturnal richness of Moonlight Music, this scene uses open daylight and candy-bright accents.

**Visual character.** Large cloud shapes and blue sky provide breathing room around many colorful islands. The plane creates a strong foreground focal point, while waterfalls and the rainbow connect otherwise separate floating zones. The image communicates curiosity, cultivation, and movement.

**Key motifs.** Flight, gardening, floating islands, windmill, rainbow, waterfalls, balloons, exploration.

### Pocket Raceway

**Artwork.** Six brightly colored sports cars travel around a single looping mountain circuit built around a turquoise lake. Pine forest, red cliffs, guardrails, track buildings, distant volcano-like peaks, and late-afternoon clouds establish a sweeping scenic setting.

**Style.** Semi-realistic cinematic automotive concept illustration. Perspective, reflections, road construction, atmospheric depth, and directional light are more naturalistic than in most of the collection, but color and landscape scale remain heightened. The polished finish evokes key art for a family-friendly racing game.

**Visual character.** The continuous dark track is the primary graphic shape, curling around the contrasting blue lake. Color-coded cars become compact puzzle landmarks. The elevated viewpoint makes the course legible as a whole and emphasizes speed without showing collision or peril.

**Key motifs.** Racing, winding road, mountain lake, colorful cars, scenic travel, motion.

### Coral Carnival

**Artwork.** A smiling whale, octopus, turtles, fish, seahorses, jellyfish, and a small yellow submarine gather around a fanciful coral city. Sunbeams, bubbles, an underwater volcano, caves, shells, seaweed, and schools of tiny fish fill the ocean from edge to edge.

**Style.** Layered cut-paper collage. Overlapping shapes have softly raised edges and subtle shadows, while waves, fins, coral, and architecture are built from simplified pieces of intensely colored “paper.” The dimensional layering gives tactile depth without relying on realistic perspective.

**Visual character.** Blue forms the base field; coral pink, orange, purple, yellow, and green are distributed evenly across it. Repetition—fish, bubbles, fronds, arches—creates rhythm and abundance. The broad whale anchors the center while smaller creatures invite a search-and-find reading.

**Key motifs.** Underwater parade, marine community, coral city, submarine, bubbles, pageantry.

### Dinosaur Valley

**Artwork.** A mixed group of gentle herbivorous dinosaurs shares a picnic near a river. A long-necked dinosaur rises above horned, plated, and armored species; juveniles gather near fruit and a checked cloth. Waterfalls, palms, cycads, mountains, a smoking volcano, pterosaurs, and a faint rainbow complete the prehistoric valley.

**Style.** Watercolor and colored-pencil natural-history illustration softened for a picture-book audience. Fine contour marks and surface hatching describe scales and plants, while transparent-looking sky and landscape washes create depth. Dinosaur anatomy is recognizable but expressions and behavior remain gentle.

**Visual character.** Earthy greens, ochres, and mineral blues support orange and yellow accents. The tall sauropod and central volcano establish a strong vertical axis; the family-sized grouping and picnic details humanize the vast landscape.

**Key motifs.** Prehistory, dinosaur families, picnic, volcano, waterfall, lush valley, peaceful coexistence.

### Little Moon Base

**Artwork.** Children and friendly round robots tend colorful flowers in lunar soil. Transparent greenhouse domes, small rovers, habitat pods, dishes, solar-flower structures, craters, Earth, Saturn, and a star-filled sky create a compact settlement devoted to growing life.

**Style.** Handcrafted clay-diorama or stop-motion miniature aesthetic. Figures, rocks, vehicles, and buildings appear modeled from softly textured material, with shallow depth of field and studio-like highlights reinforcing the sense of a physical set. Rounded machinery prevents the technology from feeling severe.

**Visual character.** Lavender-gray ground makes orange, teal, yellow, and green objects stand out. Circular forms repeat in helmets, domes, craters, robots, planets, and flowers. Foreground gardening actions keep the imaginative setting emotionally familiar.

**Key motifs.** Space settlement, gardening, children and robots, domes, rovers, planets, hopeful technology.

### Market Morning

**Artwork.** Anthropomorphic woodland animals shop, sell flowers, bread, vegetables, fruit, and toys, play an accordion, and guide ducklings through a cobbled village square. Patterned clothing, striped awnings, flower boxes, a fountain, tiled roofs, pumpkins, baskets, and patchwork fields make the town feel densely inhabited.

**Style.** Folk illustration combining linocut-like contours with tapestry and textile pattern. Forms are flattened, outlines and surface marks are prominent, and hills, roofs, garments, paving, and shop fronts are treated as decorative pattern fields. The crowded frontal staging resembles a narrative wall hanging or richly printed storybook.

**Visual character.** Amber, rust, forest green, navy, and cream create an autumnal harmony. The central fountain opens a small breathing space within the crowd; awnings, rooflines, and fields organize the density into bands. Nearly every region contains a miniature social story.

**Key motifs.** Village life, market stalls, woodland animals, harvest, music, food, community commerce.

### Aurora Camp

**Artwork.** Polar bears, cubs, penguins, Arctic foxes, seals, and a snowy owl gather around an orange tent and lanterns beneath a vast green, blue, and violet aurora. Ice arches, crystal caves, a frozen lighthouse, mountain silhouettes, a sled, and reflective water make the campsite feel both intimate and remote.

**Style.** Luminous ink-wash and chalk-pastel fantasy. The sky has flowing translucent veils, while snow, fur, and ice carry powdery marks and saturated reflected color. It is painterly, but rougher and more atmospheric than the polished finish of Moonlight Music.

**Visual character.** The warm tent is a concentrated orange counterpoint to the blue-violet ice. Lanterns repeat that warmth in small doses. The aurora spans the upper half as a grand abstract gesture, while arches, animals, and crystals supply varied landmarks below.

**Key motifs.** Arctic camp, aurora, polar wildlife, lanterns, ice formations, lighthouse, shared wonder.

### Pancake Parade

**Artwork.** A fox flips a pancake while a rabbit mixes batter and a bear, otter, hedgehog, and raccoon carry stacks or decorate plates. Berries, citrus, kiwi, syrup, bowls, cookware, plants, shelves, and a sunlit window turn breakfast preparation into a bustling ensemble performance.

**Style.** Energetic comic-book children's illustration. Strong dark contours, elastic expressions, crisp highlights, saturated local color, and frozen action give the scene a lively animated quality. The flying pancake and abundant gestures make the composition feel like a single exuberant comic panel.

**Visual character.** Character heads form an arc around the central mixing bowl; repeated pancake stacks and circular plates unify the busy foreground. Orange, yellow, turquoise, red, and berry blue keep ingredients and roles easy to distinguish.

**Key motifs.** Cooking together, breakfast, animal friends, slapstick motion, fruit, abundance.

### Table for Everyone

**Artwork.** Viewed from above, several people reach into a garden table covered with pasta, roasted vegetables, dumplings, rice, salads, hummus, flatbread, fruit, berries, sauces, and drinks. Hands enter from all sides, implying a diverse gathering beyond the frame.

**Style.** Semi-realistic painterly food illustration with an editorial or lifestyle character. Food surfaces, hands, ceramics, linen, and glass are carefully modeled, yet brush texture and warm color keep the image from becoming photographic. The overhead arrangement also borrows from still-life painting.

**Visual character.** The central platter acts as a hub, with round and oval dishes radiating around it. Reds, greens, golds, and purples are balanced over a blue-gray patterned cloth. Cropped bodies shift attention from individual identity to participation and sharing.

**Key motifs.** Communal meal, many cuisines, reaching hands, garden table, hospitality, abundance.

### Flavor Mosaic

**Artwork.** An overhead arrangement of decorated plates presents citrus, salad, watermelon, pasta, berries, bread, cherries, pastry, and croissants. Leaves and a small sun-like circle occupy gaps in a table covering constructed from bold geometric color blocks.

**Style.** Gently abstract geometric gouache still life. Visible dry-brush texture softens simplified shapes, while flattened perspective and patterned ceramics bring folk-modern and mid-century decorative influences. Foods remain immediately recognizable despite strong reduction.

**Visual character.** The composition behaves like a mosaic: circles and ovals sit over a tessellated ground of coral, teal, ultramarine, cream, mustard, and plum. There is no single protagonist; pattern, shape matching, and color relationships are the subject.

**Key motifs.** Food as pattern, overhead still life, plates, fruit, geometry, color play.

### Mud Mountain Rally

**Artwork.** Three monster trucks race across a muddy desert course. One truck launches from a striped ramp while the others carve through ruts and puddles. Monumental red-rock formations, pennant flags, barriers, clouds, flying mud, and huge tires amplify the spectacle.

**Style.** Retro screen-print adventure poster. A deliberately limited turquoise, orange, ochre, black, and cream palette combines with halftone-like grain, ink speckle, hard-edged shadows, and simplified shapes. The result has the punch of a mid-century event poster or printed toy-box art.

**Visual character.** Three trucks form a dynamic triangle, with the airborne vehicle at the apex. Diagonal ramps, tire tracks, and mud spray reinforce forward force. Flat turquoise areas sharply separate machinery from the red-orange canyon.

**Key motifs.** Monster trucks, jump, mud, desert canyon, flags, speed, mechanical spectacle.

### Dragon Meadow

**Artwork.** Five friendly young dragons play beside a stream in a flower-filled mountain meadow. One splashes in the water, another rests in the foreground, and three gather among berry bushes. A small waterfall, stepping stones, stitched-looking clouds, hills, blossoms, and warm sunlight complete the pastoral scene.

**Style.** Felted-wool and embroidery storybook art. Dragons, clouds, water, and landscape appear built from fuzzy fibers, stitched edges, knots, and layered textile pieces. The softness turns a traditionally formidable fantasy creature into something toy-like and comforting.

**Visual character.** Purple, coral, green, blue, and yellow dragons function as distinct color landmarks. The stream creates a gentle diagonal through dense floral texture. Warm cream light and tactile surfaces make the entire landscape feel handmade.

**Key motifs.** Friendly dragons, meadow, berries, stream, flowers, textile craft, gentle play.

### Playtime Park

**Artwork.** Children use slides, climbing walls, rope bridges, monkey bars, swings, seesaws, stepping blocks, a sandbox, and a perimeter climbing net in a leafy neighborhood playground. Flower borders, a multicolored fence, paths, trees, and rubber play surfaces frame the activity.

**Style.** Isometric miniature 3D illustration. An elevated, dollhouse-like viewpoint displays the whole play system at once. Softly rounded models, clean materials, controlled shadows, and carefully arranged figures suggest a premium architectural toy set or stop-motion environment.

**Visual character.** The equipment forms a central U-shaped cluster, with independent play zones clearly separated by color and geometry. Teal, yellow, coral, and natural wood repeat throughout. The viewpoint emphasizes inclusive simultaneous activity rather than one hero action.

**Key motifs.** Outdoor play, children together, climbing, swings, sandbox, miniature world, neighborhood greenery.

### Bumble Splash

**Artwork.** Six children play in and around a shallow blue flower-shaped paddling pool whose floor forms a large smiling yellow-and-black bee. A green garden hose connects visibly to the pool and powers two curved black antenna nozzles, sending sparkling fans of water high over the children. Toy boats, buckets, watering cans, striped towels, a beach ball, butterflies, daisies, sunflowers, and an excited dog extend the activity into the surrounding lawn.

**Style.** Crayon, watercolor, and wax-resist children's-book illustration. Loose grassy washes and softly mottled paper texture are overlaid with waxy white highlights, especially in the water droplets and pool rim. The handmade marks give the scene a spontaneous summer-sketchbook energy while maintaining clear faces and actions.

**Visual character.** The blue pool and oversized bee form a bold central flower-and-insect silhouette. Yellow sunflowers repeat the bee's warmth at the upper edge, while coral, purple, orange, and striped clothing distribute accents around the pool. The two high spray arcs bridge the upper half, and the connected green hose makes the playful mechanism easy to understand.

**Key motifs.** Bee, splash pool, hose-powered sprinklers, children playing, garden, summer water, toy boats, butterflies.

### Alpine Express

**Artwork.** A teal, red, and gold steam train carries animal passengers from a mountain tunnel across a tall stone viaduct and past a small station. The continuous track remains visible from the tunnel to the left edge. A bear stationmaster and young woodland animals wait safely on a raised platform behind a decorative fence and pale safety strip. A turquoise river, waterfalls, water tower, signal, snow-tipped peaks, village, sheep, cable car, flowers, deer, birds, and marmot create a richly inhabited alpine journey.

**Style.** Layered paper-quilling and paper-cut illustration. Tightly rolled and curved strips describe clouds, steam, fir branches, flowers, fleece, rippling water, and geological contours. Broader folded-paper structures shape the train, station, bridge, and mountains. Raised edges and small cast shadows make the entire world resemble an elaborate dimensional paper tableau.

**Visual character.** The railway supplies a sweeping S-curve from upper-center tunnel through the train and out of the lower-left frame. Teal, vermilion, and gold carriages form the main rhythm against pine green and lavender-gray mountains. White quilled steam, clouds, and snowy peaks repeat across the image, while the river adds a contrasting turquoise path beneath the bridge.

**Key motifs.** Steam train, continuous railway, safe station platform, mountain tunnel, viaduct, river, cable car, animal journey.

### Rocket Sunrise

**Artwork.** A silver-and-orange rocket rises from a circular desert launch pad in a bright plume of fire and curled vapor. Five young explorers and two friendly robots watch and operate instruments from behind a transparent safety barrier. Solar panels, observatory domes, radar dishes, a rover, geometric flags, flowering cacti, red mesas, birds, planets, stars, and a crescent moon fill the spaceport and dawn sky.

**Style.** Luminous stained-glass and enamel illustration. Strong dark leading divides the scene into jewel-toned panes, while translucent-looking cobalt, violet, turquoise, amber, ruby, and pearly white surfaces glow as if lit from behind. Fine irregularities in the color fields keep the finish handcrafted rather than mechanically flat.

**Visual character.** The rocket creates a commanding central vertical, balanced by an arc of spectators and scientific equipment below. Fiery amber exhaust connects the cool sky to the warm desert, and segmented clouds, planets, buildings, flags, and glass panels prevent broad empty regions. Symmetry gives the launch clarity; varied foreground silhouettes provide puzzle landmarks.

**Key motifs.** Rocket launch, young explorers, friendly robots, spaceport, stained glass, planets, teamwork, hopeful technology.

## Recurring motifs and narrative patterns

### Togetherness through activity

The most persistent motif is a group sharing an activity: sailing, playing music, gardening, picnicking, shopping, cooking, eating, racing, splashing, traveling, launching, or playing. Togetherness is shown through coordinated action, eye lines, gestures, and shared objects. This is more specific and engaging than simply placing several smiling characters in one scene.

### Nature made hospitable

Water, mountains, forests, gardens, flowers, and wildlife appear throughout the set. Wild or remote environments are repeatedly made welcoming through a small sign of care: a tent and lantern, a picnic cloth, a garden dome, a market, a playground, or a lighthouse. New works can continue this productive contrast between a large world and a secure social center.

### Gentle transformation of adventurous subjects

Dinosaurs, dragons, space exploration, racing, monster trucks, and Arctic wilderness could be intense subjects. Here they are translated into child-safe wonder through rounded forms, bright color, cooperative behavior, scenic distance, and the absence of visible harm. Energy is welcome; menace is not required.

### Abundance and collecting

Many scenes derive pleasure from plentiful variations on a category: kinds of fish, flowers, foods, dinosaurs, market goods, vehicles, or play equipment. This “many things to find” structure is especially effective for a jigsaw because each object becomes a local landmark and a prompt for conversation.

### Circles, paths, and gathering places

Circular and looping structures recur: the race circuit and lake, dinner plates, pancake stacks, greenhouse domes, planets, lanterns, bubbles, flowers, and the musicians' clearing. Paths and rivers lead through several landscapes. These devices organize a busy image without making it rigid.

### Warm light inside cool environments

Moonlight Music and Aurora Camp most clearly use golden lights against deep blue surroundings, but the principle appears elsewhere in windows, sunlit clouds, orange vehicles, and warm food. A controlled warm–cool contrast provides atmosphere and strong puzzle cues.

## Palette and composition principles

The set uses broad color variety, but most images follow three useful practices:

1. **One environmental base color.** Ocean blue, lunar lavender, forest teal, desert orange, or meadow green gives the scene coherence.
2. **Distributed accent colors.** Character clothing, vehicles, flowers, food, lights, and architecture repeat contrasting hues across the frame rather than concentrating all color in one corner.
3. **Distinct local regions.** Sky, ground, water, buildings, foliage, and foreground objects remain separable even when the scene is dense.

Compositions generally combine a large readable anchor with numerous smaller discoveries. Successful anchors include the boat, musicians, plane, race circuit, whale, sauropod, gardeners, fountain, tent, mixing bowl, central platter, plate mosaic, airborne truck, stream, and playground structure. This balance keeps thumbnails legible while preserving interest at 96 or 192 pieces.

- Food, sharing, and community.
- Water, gardens, mountains, and pastoral nature.
- Fantasy, prehistory, space, railways, vehicles, and everyday play.
- Bright daylight, golden daylight, and luminous night.

## Opportunities for expansion

Future scenes should add at least one genuinely new dimension—subject, environment, culture, season, viewpoint, material language, or type of activity—rather than merely reskinning an existing composition.

Promising gaps include:

- **Seasons and weather:** autumn rain, a snowy town, spring planting, puddle play, or a cozy storm viewed from shelter.
- **Habitats:** desert night, rainforest canopy, wetlands, caves, high mountains, or an insect-scale garden.
- **Everyday making:** pottery studio, community workshop, sewing room, library, theatre rehearsal, or science fair.
- **Celebration and performance:** lantern festival, outdoor dance, puppet show, kite gathering, or neighborhood parade, handled without culture-specific clichés.
- **New visual media:** woodblock print, mosaic tile, paper marbling, paper quilling used for a non-landscape subject, crayon and wax resist used for a quieter mood, risograph, blocky low-poly 3D, ink line with limited wash, or ceramic and glaze effects.
- **New viewpoints:** a cutaway building, side-on procession, close low-angle garden, symmetrical stage, or map-like journey.
- **Quieter moods:** reading, observing wildlife, building something slowly, or resting after an adventure. Calm should still include enough visual landmarks for puzzle play.
- **Human-centered worlds:** the collection includes children and hands, but most narrative characters are animals. More varied groups of children, families, ages, abilities, and appearances could broaden representation while retaining the same warm ensemble spirit.

## Guidance for new artwork

### Preserve

- A generous, optimistic emotional tone.
- A clear shared activity or discoverable story.
- One strong compositional anchor and several secondary points of interest.
- Useful detail across the full landscape frame, including corners and edges.
- Distinct shapes, textures, or color zones that remain identifiable when divided into puzzle pieces.
- Original, unbranded characters and objects.
- A clean image with no writing, logos, watermark, border, frame, or painted jigsaw seams.

### Vary

- Character type and number.
- Geographic or imaginary environment.
- Time of day, season, and weather.
- Camera position and spatial organization.
- Simulated material and mark-making.
- Dominant palette and color temperature.
- Pace, from peaceful observation to energetic action.

### Avoid

- Repeating the exact formula of cute animals gathered around food.
- A single oversized face or object against an empty background.
- Large low-detail skies, walls, floors, or bodies that produce frustrating puzzle regions.
- Uniform micro-detail with no larger shapes to organize it.
- Muddy values that make adjacent areas indistinguishable.
- Unmotivated spectacle, danger, aggression, or visual chaos.
- Direct imitation of a named living artist or recognizable copyrighted property.

## A practical concept template

Each proposal for a new scene can be defined in one sentence and six supporting decisions:

> **[Characters]** are **[shared activity]** in **[distinct environment]**, shown in a **[viewpoint/composition]** and rendered with the tactile character of **[artistic medium or graphic tradition]**.

Then specify:

1. **Story anchor:** the main action readable at thumbnail size.
2. **Discovery motifs:** five to ten secondary objects or mini-stories.
3. **Style distinction:** how the marks, edges, depth, and texture differ from current works.
4. **Palette plan:** one base color family plus two or three distributed accent colors.
5. **Puzzle structure:** major regions, recognizable silhouettes, and detailed edges/corners.
6. **Collection contribution:** the new subject, mood, representation, or technique it adds.

For example:

> A multigenerational group builds kites together on a breezy hilltop, shown as a sweeping side-on procession and rendered like translucent stained tissue paper with visible bamboo ribs.

This proposal preserves collaboration, nature, color, and motion while adding a multigenerational human cast, a wind-focused story, a lateral composition, and a new translucent material language.

## Review checklist

Before adding a scene, ask:

- Is the activity understandable without a title?
- Does the image offer a fresh style or subject relative to the eighteen existing scenes?
- Is there a focal anchor as well as detail throughout the frame?
- Are the corners and broad background regions interesting enough to assemble?
- Can major regions be distinguished by color, value, texture, or shape?
- Does the scene reward looking closely with multiple small discoveries?
- Is the mood inviting and suitable for children without becoming visually bland?
- Are characters and objects original, inclusive where applicable, and free of branding?
- Is the final asset landscape-oriented, free of text and seams, and technically consistent with the game?

The collection will remain coherent if new works preserve its emotional and narrative principles while continuing to broaden its visual languages. The goal is not to make every picture look alike; it is to make every picture feel like another welcoming world worth assembling.
